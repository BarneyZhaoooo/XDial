XDial 隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 17 日 · 适用于 XDial iOS 版与 macOS 版

XDial 是一款网络分流工具，由 XD Entertainment Pte Ltd 发布。它在你的设备上建立本地 VPN，按你设置的场景和规则，把网络流量分别直连或交给你配置的线路。

我们不收集你的数据

  • XDial 没有账号系统，不含统计、广告或追踪 SDK，也不向我们的服务器上传任何信息。
  • 我们看不到你访问了哪些网站，也看不到你的流量内容。

保存在你设备上的信息

  • 线路、场景、规则和订阅等配置只保存在你的设备上；线路密码等凭据保存在系统钥匙串中。
  • 为了按网络自动选择场景，XDial 会在本机读取当前 Wi‑Fi 名称，不会发送到任何地方。
  • 灵动岛和实时活动里显示的网速只在本机计算。

XDial 自己发出的网络请求

  • 你的流量：按你的配置直连，或发往你配置的线路服务器（例如所在单位的 VPN）。这些服务器由其运营者管理，适用其运营者的隐私政策。
  • 判断出口地区：自动选择场景时，XDial 会访问 Apple（gspe1-ssl.ls.apple.com）和 Cloudflare（/cdn-cgi/trace）的公开接口，读取当前网络出口所在的国家或地区。
  • 连接自检：连接后会访问 Google、Apple 和 Cloudflare 的公开测试地址，确认网络和分流是否生效。
  • 规则与订阅更新：从你配置的规则集和订阅地址下载更新。

和所有网络请求一样，这些服务会看到你的 IP 地址；XDial 不会附带任何能识别你身份的信息。

儿童

XDial 不面向儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会更新在本页面。如有问题，请在 github.com/xindong/XDial/issues 提交。

XDial Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026 · Applies to XDial for iOS and macOS

XDial is a network routing tool published by XD Entertainment Pte Ltd. It runs a local VPN on your device and, following the scenarios and rules you set, sends each connection either directly or through the lines you configure.

We do not collect your data

  • XDial has no accounts and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. It uploads nothing to our servers.
  • We cannot see which sites you visit or the content of your traffic.

Information kept on your device

  • Lines, scenarios, rules and subscriptions are stored only on your device; credentials such as line passwords are kept in the system Keychain.
  • To pick a scenario for the current network, XDial reads the Wi‑Fi name on the device. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The speeds shown in the Dynamic Island and Live Activity are computed on the device.

Network requests XDial makes

  • Your traffic goes directly or to the line servers you configured (for example your organization's VPN). Those servers are run by their operators, whose privacy policies apply.
  • Exit region: when choosing a scenario automatically, XDial queries public endpoints of Apple (gspe1-ssl.ls.apple.com) and Cloudflare (/cdn-cgi/trace) to learn the country or region of the current network exit.
  • Connection self-check: after connecting, XDial requests public test URLs from Google, Apple and Cloudflare to confirm that the network and routing work.
  • Rule and subscription updates are downloaded from the addresses you configured.

As with any network request, these services see your IP address; XDial adds nothing that identifies you.

Children

XDial is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/xindong/XDial/issues.
